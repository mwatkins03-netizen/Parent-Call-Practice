Practice lab · Live voice role-play

Practice the hard parent calls before they're real.

You'll talk with an AI role-play parent. Sometimes you make the call with hard news; sometimes the parent calls you upset, in a hurry, or sure they know better; once in a while they're simply on your side. The parent reacts the way real parents do. Captions run live the whole time, and afterward you get a transcript, feedback, and a reflection to turn in.

  1. Choose a scenarioPick from 35 situations, choose the kind of parent on the line, and set how hard the call should be.
  2. PrepareRead the teacher goal, then plan your opening, your key message, and the next step you'll offer.
  3. Make the callTalk out loud, or type. Live captions show both voices. Calls last up to 10 minutes.
  4. DebriefReview the transcript and feedback, rate yourself, reflect, and export.

Advanced: model settings

What to expect

  • The parent is an AI. It's a practice partner, not a real person, and it can get things wrong.
  • Live captions and a running transcript stay on screen. You can type any line instead of saying it.
  • Need a moment? Take a break pauses the call. You can end it at any time.
  • Nothing is uploaded except the call audio, which goes to Google in Live mode. Your report saves only when you export it.
