Practice lab · Live voice role-play

Practice the call you'll dread making.

You'll call an AI role-play parent and tell them something hard: a failing grade, a behavior incident, an injury. The parent reacts the way real parents do. Captions run live the whole time, and afterward you get a transcript, feedback, and a reflection to turn in.

  1. Choose a scenarioPick the news, the parent's mood, and how hard the call should be.
  2. PreparePlan your opening line, the facts, and the next step you'll offer.
  3. Make the callTalk out loud, or type. Live captions show both voices. Calls last up to 10 minutes.
  4. DebriefReview the transcript and feedback, rate yourself, reflect, and export.

Advanced: model settings

What to expect

  • The parent is an AI. It's a practice partner, not a real person, and it can get things wrong.
  • Live captions and a running transcript stay on screen. You can type any line instead of saying it.
  • Need a moment? Take a break pauses the call. You can end it at any time.
  • Nothing is uploaded except the call audio, which goes to Google in Live mode. Your report saves only when you export it.
